# ADR-004: Strict Professional Content Isolation

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Blueprint may explore engineering topics that overlap with knowledge acquired during a professional career. Publishing identifiable or proprietary professional material would undermine the product and could create confidentiality risks.

## Decision

The Blueprint repository is the authoritative project context. Agents and contributors must not use unrelated private repositories or professional material as source content.

All Blueprint implementations and examples must be independently created from public knowledge and original work. Renaming proprietary concepts is not sufficient.

## Consequences

Some useful examples must be recreated from first principles. When provenance is uncertain, material is excluded. Blueprint remains an engineering lab rather than an indirect representation of employer/client work.
