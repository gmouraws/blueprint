# ADR-001: Engineering Lab, Not Portfolio

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

A traditional personal portfolio centers employment history, skill lists, screenshots, and recruiter-oriented project summaries. Blueprint is intended to demonstrate engineering thinking through ongoing work.

## Decision

Blueprint will be structured as an engineering lab with Builds, Experiments, and Notes. It will not contain an employment timeline, client showcase, or traditional portfolio section.

## Consequences

The site can evolve continuously and prioritize technical substance. Professional credibility should emerge from the quality of the work rather than résumé-style presentation.
