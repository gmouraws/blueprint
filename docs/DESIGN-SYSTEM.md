# Blueprint Room presentation

A restrained dark shared space with a small three-bar room mark, clear text and mint accents. No copied Discord branding, fictitious participants, decorative landing sections, animation or gradients.

Tokens: background #10151b, surface #181f28, text #edf1f5, muted #a6b2c1, accent #a3e1c5, dividers #303b48. System sans-serif for reading; monospace only for small labels. Controls have at least 44px height, visible focus and explicit labels. Status is textual as well as colored.

Entry: one compact, centered nickname/password card with the direct title "Entrar na sala" and the room capacity and capabilities below it. Preserve the brand/private-space header, dark palette and existing form treatment. No slogans, promotional column or repeated branding. Permission guidance stays beside the entry action. Invalid configuration disables entry and provides expandable diagnostics containing only variable names and safe status categories. Room: text/voice navigation and participants on the left, with accessible Sala/Chat tabs in the main area. Sala prioritizes screen viewing; Chat uses the full conversation width. The selected view and actual voice connection are labeled separately. Call controls and audio settings remain available in both views. On smaller screens the sidebar becomes compact above the tabs, and chat history scrolls within a bounded panel so the composer stays reachable. Long nicknames/messages wrap. No horizontal page overflow at 320px.

The old Technical Editorial website is retained only in Git history. Its mark, navigation, biographies, articles and marketing assets are no longer served.

The room workspace fills the available viewport. Compact headers and one-line, auto-growing chat input reserve the flexible area for messages; retention and input guidance remain accessible in the chat information popover. Attachment and send actions retain 44px targets and accessible names. Selected-image/upload feedback appears only when needed.

Screen viewing defaults to a centered, aspect-preserving image bounded by the received resolution and available space. Optional "Ajustar à área" may enlarge it; fullscreen remains in the viewing menu. The selected-screen mute and volume are always visible beside a dark popover selector with native radio keyboard behavior. Streams without audio say "Sem áudio". The compact toolbar overlays the image without resizing it and supports keyboard and touch. Fullscreen retains microphone and incoming-audio controls.

The voice heading is simply "Voz", with combined capacity in its tooltip and accessible text. The connected room uses mint and an accessible state. The outlined room label marks the room being browsed; a separate mint speaker identifies the joined voice room. Room labels select on one click and join on double click. Disclosure remains a separate 44px-high target. The selected-room header provides the compact accessible join action. Microphone and incoming-audio buttons sit together in that order in every view. The microphone uses real SDK state, a red crossed icon when off and disabled/pending feedback during changes; incoming mute has its own crossed headphones and amber pressed treatment.

Own messages have a discreet options menu and a modal confirmation with Cancel focused first. Removing an image preserves text. Audio settings expose browser-native noise suppression and report actual track settings or lack of support; no visual claim of active processing is inferred from the preference alone.

Voice membership notices use compact floating text notices above the composer/call controls, naming the participant and room without shrinking chat or video. A polite live log announces additions without moving focus; up to three notices expire after 12 seconds and can be dismissed by keyboard/touch. No animation, system notification permission or additional notification sound is introduced.

Phase A adds a third compact audio action for combined mute to the same footer/viewer control group. Settings explain restoration and offer optional Ctrl+Shift+letter bindings, clear/reset and conflict feedback. The microphone and deafen remain separately labeled. Own-image deletion uses a small adjacent trash icon with a 44px target, accessible label/title and red hover/focus. Desktop hover/focus reveals it; touch keeps it visible. Message-menu deletion, confirmation and server ownership semantics are unchanged.
